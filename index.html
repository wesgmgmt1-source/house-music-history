The History of House Music

How a Chicago club sound became a worldwide movement

Where It Started

House music was born in Chicago in the early 1980s. DJs at clubs like the Warehouse mixed disco, soul, and electronic sounds into long, nonstop sets that kept people dancing all night.

Frankie Knuckles, the Warehouse's resident DJ, is often called the "Godfather of House." The name "house" is usually traced back to the Warehouse itself.

What Makes It House

Most house tracks share a few key ingredients:

  • A steady four-on-the-floor kick drum
  • A tempo around 120 to 130 beats per minute
  • Drum machines like the Roland TR-808 and TR-909
  • Soulful vocals and repeating piano or synth lines
