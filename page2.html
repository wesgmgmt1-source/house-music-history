Songs and Albums That Shaped House

The records that took house from Chicago to the world

How House Spread

House grew from a few Chicago clubs into a global genre in about a decade.

  1. Early 1980s: Chicago DJs build the sound at clubs like the Warehouse and the Music Box.
  2. 1984: Jesse Saunders releases "On and On," one of the first house records.
  3. 1986 to 1987: Chicago labels like Trax and DJ International bring house to the UK.
  4. Late 1980s: House takes over UK clubs and raves.
  5. 1990s: House spreads worldwide, from New York garage to French house.

Key Songs

These tracks helped define the early Chicago sound:

  • "Your Love" by Frankie Knuckles and Jamie Principle
  • "Move Your Body" by Marshall Jefferson (1986)
  • "Can You Feel It" by Mr. Fingers (1986)
  • "Jack Your Body" by Steve "Silk" Hurley (1986)

Key Albums

These albums carried house to bigger audiences:

  • Another Side by Fingers Inc. (1988)
  • Homework by Daft Punk (1997)
  • Remedy by Basement Jaxx (1999)
